# Ancestry World — Update Log

Also available in-game: **Main menu → What's New**.

## v0.7.2 — Tools, crafting benches and a more varied people (2026-11-27)

- **New:** Personal tools and weapons: everyone carries their own kit of axes, pickaxes, swords, fishing rods, shovels and sickles, in wood, stone, iron or gold. Tools make their work much faster (chopping, mining, building, sowing, harvesting, fishing), swords hit harder in fights and hunts, and every tool wears out and breaks with use.
- **New:** Crafting Bench: an early building where crafters (or anyone, when nobody else can) make tools for whoever lacks them. Wood is cheap and quick to wear out, stone is the early workhorse, iron (after Iron Working, from iron ore) is the best, and gold is fast but soft. Finished tools go on the store shelf and people fetch upgrades for their trade.
- **New:** You can see the kit: people hold the actual tool they are using, fishers cast a line with a bobbing float, and the citizen window's Gear tab lists each tool with its wear.
- **Improved:** Much more varied people: shirts, trousers, bodices, corsets, dresses, skirts, socks and boots are mixed and matched independently, linen is dyed in earthy tones early and bright colours later, and there are more hats, caps, gloves, face paint, packs and hairstyles.
- **Fixed:** Backpacks are drawn behind the body instead of over it.
- **Improved:** Hair mixes and matches too: any hairstyle can now come in any hair colour (black, browns, auburn, ginger, blonde, platinum, and the rare blue, pink or green), following each person's inherited hair colour, and people go grey with age.

## v0.7.1 — Orders, the divine hand and living characters (2026-11-27)

- **New:** Orders replace the Possess button on the left (key Y): pick Fight, Chop, Mine, Hunt, Deconstruct or Destroy and click a target, and your best-suited people go and do it. Chopping and mining work through the whole grove or vein around the spot, carrying loads home between trips. Deconstructing returns half the materials; you can only destroy enemy buildings when at war. Possessing still works from a citizen's window or the F key.
- **New:** The divine hand: press and hold on a person to pick them up. They kick in the air while you carry them; let go gently to set them down, or flick the mouse to throw them (hard throws hurt). You can lift your own people and those of computer nations, never another player's without permission. Works in co-op.
- **New:** The new character models got their full animation set: running when fleeing or charging, jumping for joy when a building is finished or a new era begins, kicking while held, a proper collapse when someone dies, and a lying pose for the sleeping, downed and dead.
- **Fixed:** Knocked-over and killed people no longer turn back into the old sprites while they tumble: the ragdoll now uses the new character art.
- **Fixed:** Half-zoomed-out views no longer show hard cut edges at the poles or overlapping map layers while blending into the globe.
- **Fixed:** Windows now open in free space and never cover the Stores list, the toolbar, the power bar or each other.
- **Fixed:** Scrolling inside the Civilization Book and other windows no longer jumps back to the top when the window refreshes.
- **Improved:** The book's paper animation plays once per page: the first time you open the book and the first time you visit each chapter.
- **Improved:** No more ragdolls: knocked-over people hop, fall and get back up using their own character animations, and the dead collapse with a proper death animation.
- **Balance:** New peoples start with almost nothing, just a few handfuls of berries, and must gather everything else. New towns only get what their settlers carry over from the old one.
- **Fixed:** People no longer carry a basket around forever: they empty it at the store before taking new work, and a load that goes undelivered is set down for the haulers.

## v0.7.0 — Items, professions, a research tree and towns that grow up (2026-11-27)

- **New:** Everything drops real goods: trees give logs, rocks give stone, ore gives copper, iron or gold, bushes give berries, apples or bananas by biome, fields grow climate-suited crops (wheat, carrots, corn, tomatoes, cotton and more), cows give red meat and hides, sheep give wool, chickens give poultry and eggs, fish come in two kinds. Cooks turn them into bread, roasts, pastries, meals or rations.
- **New:** Goods you can see: carried items float over people, possessed harvests and wild carcasses drop on the ground and haulers fetch them, and the storehouse shows its stock piled in front of it. A Stores list on the right shows everything you own, grouped by kind.
- **Fixed:** Buildings are no longer free: builders carry the materials to the site first. The placement preview shows what a building costs (red when you are short) and every construction site shows the materials still missing above it.
- **Fixed:** Mines now work: miners dig stone, copper, iron and the odd gold nugget there endlessly. Possessed mining, chopping and harvesting drop the goods at your feet.
- **New:** Professions: every adult specialises in their best skill (Builder, Farmer, Hunter, Miner, Cook, Crafter, Scholar, Doctor, Priest) and sticks to it, while the settlement makes sure every needed trade is covered. Everyone pitches in for food when stores run low.
- **New:** Research tree: six technologies per era across all ten eras. Pick what your scholars study; finish four of an era's techs and its capstone to enter the next era. Techs boost yields, research, Glitter, mood, healing, construction and movement, and unlock buildings.
- **New:** Hide Tailoring: once researched, everyone sews themselves an outfit at the new Tailor, and again whenever a new era brings better clothes.
- **New:** New buildings: Tailor, Chicken Coop (daily eggs) and Well.
- **New:** Towns grow up with the eras using LimeZu Modern Farm and Modern Exteriors art: tents, barns and farmhouses, then a palace, Victorian streets, a clock-tower town hall, terraced houses, offices, a hospital, school, malls, condos, hangars and an observatory. Shrines are now statues, then an angel, a chapel and finally an animated fountain. Elves and orcs recolour their towns and name their buildings their own way.
- **New:** Street furniture appears around settlements by era: hay and crates, then carts and flower beds, then lamps, benches, mailboxes, phone booths, EV chargers and solar panels.
- **Improved:** The plan menu shows each building's picture, what it does, its stats and its cost. Clicking a building opens its own window with materials delivered so far.
- **New:** The Nation window is now a Civilization Book (Humble Gift Paper UI): bookmarks for Chronicle, Research, Creed and Diplomacy, your people on the left page, the chapter on the right. The Codex uses the same paper style.
- **Improved:** Days last four times longer, so people have time to work between nights; needs, ageing, births and crops follow the calendar. At higher speeds creatures now animate faster too, and the simulation keeps up through slow frames.
- **Improved:** Fewer mental breaks: the risk is judged a few times per calendar day instead of constantly.
- **Fixed:** A two-person start can no longer be softlocked by an infertile founder.
- **Balance:** Powers are stronger: bigger brushes, full healing, stronger rain, instant crop blessing, more summoned animals, harder-hitting smites, and buffs that last real days.
- **Fixed:** Clicking a citizen now uses the exact area their sprite covers.
- **Improved:** Animals are smaller and far fewer. Kinds without their own sprite (wolves, bears, deer, boars, camels, elephants, mammoths, songbirds) and their powers are gone; foxes now live wild in forests.
- **Fixed:** Chickens no longer flicker or show stray text: the broken rooster sheets were removed.
- **Fixed:** Flags no longer flicker in menus (the same flag picture was being moved between windows).
- **Fixed:** Stray pumpkins no longer appear in the grass.
- **Improved:** The Civilization Book now uses the paper pack's own animation: pages appear as a blank sheet dissolves into writing when you open it or turn to a new chapter, the pages sit inside the book desk, and the bookmarks hang from the right page.
- **Fixed:** Early huts now always use the hide-tent art (they fell back to the procedural tent), including while being built.
- **Improved:** The Elder Fire is the campfire burning tall, and the Healer's Tent is the hide tent with a red cross and a medkit.

## v0.6.0 — Creeds, bigger worlds, 31 new powers and a new Codex (2026-11-27)

- **New:** Creeds replace Divine Will: every nation holds a few Ideals (Spiritual, Pacifist, Industrialist, Stewardship...), six Precepts (war, toil, land, the dead, outsiders, knowledge) and rites it performs on its own. Certainty rises and falls with prophets, temples and happiness; low certainty brings doubt, conversion to rival creeds, or a schism. Revelation restores certainty and lets you reform at once.
- **Improved:** The Nation window is larger and the Creed & Will tab is laid out with ideal cards, precept selectors, rituals and tendency bars.
- **New:** 31 new powers across every tab: Benediction, Gift of Tongues, Consecration, Great Awakening, Grove of Life, Rainbow, Wetland, Oasis, Vigor, Muse, Manna, Wild Boar, Camels, Elephants, Woolly Mammoth, War Horn, Thunderclap, Holy Wrath, Nightmares, Lightning Strike, Heat Wave, Cold Snap, Wildfire, Acid Rain, Madness, Raise Island, Flood, Earthquake, Meteor, Tsunami and Volcano. Each has its own icon.
- **Balance:** New powers are priced between the old tiers (small gifts 14-30 Glitter, wonders 45-140, big disasters 240-330) and the heavy ones rest for 8-20 days. Harmful powers only touch wild land, your own land, or nations that granted you miracles; war powers only hurt people at war with you.
- **Improved:** Co-op: every new power runs on the host like the rest, so the results (terrain, buildings, creatures, moods, cooldowns, Glitter) reach every player in sync.
- **New:** Bigger worlds: Small, Normal, Large and Huge planets (up to 1536x768), with more starting nations as the planet grows.
- **New:** World generation archetypes (Pangaea, archipelago, twin continents, ice age, water world, and more) with randomised ice caps, climate, rivers, lakes and volcanoes, so no two worlds look alike.
- **Improved:** Coastlines are smoothed: shores curve in rounded bays and headlands with a sand and foam fringe instead of stair-steps where land meets water.
- **Improved:** Possession: the Talk action is gone, and the hint line no longer overlaps the action bar. The citizen Skills tab now shows only the top few skills, with a "Show all" toggle.
- **New:** A Focus button appears when you zoom out or wander far from home. Click it (or press H) to fly back to your settlement.
- **Fixed:** Pine trees no longer carry a loose trunk or stump piece beside them (the "half-cut" trees), and the clothesline sheets are gone for good.
- **Improved:** Boulders, ore veins and berry bushes now reuse the pack's own rocks and bushes (with fruit dots and ore flecks added), so they match the trees. Stray pumpkins no longer sprout in the grass.
- **New:** Living wildlife: the whole planet is now seeded with herds by biome (cattle, horses, sheep, goats, pigs, junglefowl, ducks, rabbits, wolves and boar). They graze, breed (young are smaller until grown), age and die, stay below a per-species cap, and migrants refill hunted-out regions. Previously only a few animals appeared near settlements.
- **Improved:** Every animal now wears the Modern Farm sprites (wolves, boar, deer, camels, elephants and mammoths reuse them at different sizes); the old procedural animal art is gone. Co-op: each player is sent only the animals near their view, so big maps stay fast.
- **Improved:** The Codex is rebuilt as a sidebar reference with icons, searchable cards and filters: Welcome, Powers (by tab and tier), Creeds, Traits, Races, Eras, Governments, World Ages, Biomes and Credits.

## v0.5.0 — Mountains, new lands and moonlight (2026-11-27)

- **New:** Mountain ranges: tall chunky peaks with snow caps rise out of the high ground, and hill-shading makes every slope, ridge and valley read at a glance across the whole map.
- **New:** 10 new biomes: Steppe, Red Badlands, Blossom Grove, Autumn Woods, Temperate Rainforest, Mangrove Coast, Salt Flats, Alpine Meadow, Ashlands (around volcanic rifts) and Glowcap Fen. Each has its own ground art, trees, wildlife and resources.
- **Improved:** Smarter world generation: warped coastlines, mountain chains that grow along ridge lines inland, foothills that step down from the peaks, island arcs off the continents, patchier biome regions and a smoothing pass that removes lone speckle tiles.
- **Improved:** Land borders blend softly into each other instead of stair-stepping, and all trees are now recolours of the existing tree sprites (autumn reds, blossom pinks, redwood greens, frosted, ashen...).
- **New:** Dynamic lighting: a golden-hour glow at sunrise and sunset, deep blue nights, flickering fires, and people carrying a little light after dark.
- **New:** Possession actions: punch, swing, chop, mine and harvest. Pick one with the action bar or keys 1-6, then press F or click. You never harm your own people or another deity's.
- **New:** Cloud saves only list Ancestry World saves. Start with a founding pair (or a small family or full band) of a random race. Farm and Wild creature tabs, and a themed scrollbar on the power dock.
- **Improved:** Pausing now freezes every animation. The zoom out to the planet is slower and ends with a planet-reveal animation, and the flat map is shown once instead of repeating.
- **Fixed:** Removed stray dark dashes on the grass, half-cut bushes at chunk edges and the floating clotheslines.
